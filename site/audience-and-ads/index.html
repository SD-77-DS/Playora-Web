Target Audience & Ads

Target audience

Playora is a general-purpose utility app for users aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children and does not contain content aimed at them. In Play Console, choose the 13+ age groups.

Ads declaration

Playora contains no advertisements. In Play Console, answer "No" to "Does your app contain ads?".

If you add AdMob or any ad SDK later, update this declaration, the data safety form and the privacy policy before releasing the update.
