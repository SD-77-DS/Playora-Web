Permissions Explained

Justifications for the Play Console declarations. Last updated November 16, 2026

Foreground service: media playback

FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Why it is essential: Playora's core function is playing audio and video. When you start playback and then minimize the app or lock your phone, Android needs a foreground service of type mediaPlayback to keep playing. Without it, the system would stop playback as soon as the screen turns off.

  1. Open a video or song in Playora and start playing.
  2. Minimize the app or lock the phone.
  3. Playback continues and can be controlled from the notification and lock screen.

The service runs only while media is playing or paused by the user, and stops when the app is closed with nothing playing. It does nothing else.

Photo and video permissions

READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+), and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE up to Android 12L

Why it is essential: A media player must show the user's local library so they can pick something to play. Playora cannot offer this using the system photo picker alone, because its job is to browse and play whole libraries of video and music, with folders, albums, artists and queues.

  1. The user opens Playora and is asked for media access.
  2. The user grants permission.
  3. Playora lists the videos and music available on the device.
  4. The user selects an item and Playora plays it.

Media is read only on the device and is never uploaded. Playora does not request access to photos or images. It also works without the permission for files opened from other apps or share sheets.

Google Play restricts broad media permissions. A media player is a relevant core use case, but approval is not guaranteed, so describe this flow clearly in the declaration form.

Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Why: To show the media playback notification, so you can control Playora without opening it:

  • Play / Pause
  • Next and previous track
  • Playback information (title, artist and artwork)

Playora does not send marketing or promotional notifications.
