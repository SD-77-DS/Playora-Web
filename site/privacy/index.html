Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Playora is a media player. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it does with that information and how to reach us.

1. What information Playora accesses

  • Local video and audio files. With your permission, Playora reads the media stored on your device so you can browse and play it.
  • Files or links you open. When you open a file from another app, share a file to Playora or enter a URL, Playora reads only that item to play it.
  • Playback state. Playback position, history, playlists and your settings are saved on your device only so playback can resume.

2. Personal information

Playora does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, sign-in or registration, and it does not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

3. Sharing with third parties

Playora does not sell or share your information with anyone. It does not upload your media files or your listening and viewing history to any server.

4. How local media files are accessed

Playora uses Android's media permissions (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_MEDIA_AUDIO on Android 13+, and storage read access on older versions) to list and play the media you choose. Files are read on your device and are never copied off it. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings, and files opened through another app need no broad permission.

5. Network streams

If you enter a URL, Playora connects directly from your device to the server hosting that stream, as any player or browser would. That server may receive your IP address and normal request information. Playora has no control over third-party servers, so review their privacy policies.

6. Analytics, advertising and crash reporting

Playora includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking. If this changes, this policy and the Play Store declarations will be updated first.

7. Data retention and deletion

Everything Playora stores (history, playlists, settings) lives on your device. Clear it from Android Settings → Apps → Playora → Storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Children

Playora is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, write to the contact page.
